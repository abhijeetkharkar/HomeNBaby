## 👶 Part 3: Baby Setup & Essentials Checklist

### Baby Safe Home Prep
- [ ] **Furniture Anchors:** Anchor dressers, bookshelves, and TV stands to the wall (crucial!).
- [ ] **Outlet Covers:** Install in all accessible outlets.
- [ ] **Cabinet Locks:** Install on lower kitchen/bathroom cabinets (especially where cleaning supplies are stored).
- [ ] **Corner Guards:** Apply to sharp coffee tables or TV units.
- [ ] **Cords:** Tie up blind cords out of reach; manage loose electrical wires.

### Baby Supplies Essentials List
*Keep it simple; you can always buy more via Amazon once the baby arrives.*
- [ ] **Feeding:** Bottles (start with 2-4 of different brands to see what baby likes), bottle brush, drying rack, formula (1 small tub just in case), burp cloths (10+).
- [ ] **Diapering:** Diapers (1 box Newborn, 2 boxes Size 1), unscented wipes, diaper rash cream (e.g., Aquaphor/Desitin), diaper pail & bags, changing pad.
- [ ] **Clothing:** Onesies (zip-up are best for night changes! 5-7 NB size, 5-7 0-3M size), 2-3 swaddles/sleep sacks, socks, mittens.
- [ ] **Health/Hygiene:** Baby bath tub, gentle baby wash, baby lotion, nail file/electric trimmer, thermometer (rectal is most accurate for infants), bulb syringe/FridaBaby snot sucker, infant Tylenol (for later).
- [ ] **Gear:** Car seat (installed and inspected), stroller, crib/bassinet.
